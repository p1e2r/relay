Relay Privacy Policy

Edit this page before publishing: replace the operator name and contact email.

Relay is a self-hosted messenger. Each Relay server is run by its operator ([operator name], contact: [email]). The Relay app only talks to the server address you enter when signing in.

What the server stores

  • Your phone number (to create your account and send login codes by SMS).
  • Your profile: name, About and profile photo.
  • Contacts you add (their phone numbers and the names you give them).
  • Messages, media, polls, reactions and call history in your chats, so they can be delivered to your devices.
  • Your signed-in devices and when they were last active.

What the app uses on your device

  • Microphone and camera: only for calls, voice messages and photos you choose to send.
  • Location: only when you choose to share your location in a chat.
  • Notifications: to alert you to new messages.

Relay has no ads, analytics or third-party trackers. Data is not sold or shared, except with the SMS provider used to deliver your login code.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit with HTTPS. Messages are stored on the operator's server and are not end-to-end encrypted.

Deleting your data

Delete your account in the app under Settings → Security → Delete my account. This removes your profile, contacts, direct chats and group memberships. Messages that disappear or that you delete for everyone are removed from the server. You can also email the operator to request deletion.
